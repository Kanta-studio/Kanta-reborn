KANTA Android privacy — reconstruction preview

Your library stays under your control. Selected MIDI/KAR, MP3/WAV, SF2, photos and videos are copied into app-private storage without rewriting the originals. Copies retain their original metadata. Song titles, singer names, reservations, saved sets and visual selections are stored locally. Android backup is disabled for this preview.

The app has no KANTA account, advertising SDK or analytics service. Its manifest requests no internet or broad shared-storage permission. Normal local playback and library search work offline.

Front camera is optional. KANTA requests CAMERA permission only after you choose to turn on the front-camera stage. Only a live preview is bound: there is no capture, analysis or recording use case, and no camera frames are saved or uploaded. Leaving the foreground turns it off; returning does not turn it back on. You may revoke camera permission in Android Settings. The camera view can move between the primary and fullscreen stage; an external display uses a still background.

Choosing a Scenic preset stops the personal visual but keeps your last private photo/video for reuse. Replacing a photo or video removes its previous same-kind private copy after the new selection commits. Clear KANTA's app storage or uninstall to remove the local library and visual copies; keep your original music and exports backed up elsewhere.

Voice Search opens the device's selected speech-recognition provider. That provider may process audio online under its own terms. KANTA receives recognized search text and does not automatically reserve a song. Optional wired/USB monitoring and local Dry/Room/Hall/Echo effects are described below. Pitch correction and companion networking are not included. Optional pitch-following estimates are described below.

Microphone input is optional. KANTA requests RECORD_AUDIO only when you turn on the microphone. Dry PCM exists only in bounded memory for input-level and monophonic pitch estimates; no microphone audio is saved or uploaded. Input capture does not automatically enable monitored output. Input stops on backgrounding, audio focus loss or an interrupted capture; returning does not enable it again. Android may apply device processing, silence capture, or revoke permission. Input estimates can hear backing music and do not identify a singer. Song scoring requires its separate opt-in and a supported melody reference. Turn it off or revoke permission in Android Settings at any time.

Monitoring is a separate explicit choice for connected wired/USB or analog-line equipment. Use headphones and start with low equipment volume. KANTA starts muted while checking actual routing; speaker, Bluetooth and unknown reported routes are rejected. Android 16 and newer report all routed outputs and KANTA rejects more than one. Older Android versions expose only one routed output, so additional unreported outputs cannot be verified. A detected output change or disconnect disables that activation, and reconnecting does not enable it again. Android routing callbacks and physical equipment behavior are not an absolute guarantee against transient feedback; physical route/latency/quality testing is still required. The monitor has its own bounded in-memory buffer and optional Dry/Room/Hall/Echo processing. Effects do not change the separate dry pitch analysis. No monitored audio is saved or uploaded, and settings cannot enable output by themselves. Leaving KANTA, stopping input or losing audio focus stops monitoring.

Song scoring is off by default for a new app session. It uses the existing dry pitch estimate, never the monitored effects output. A single identified monophonic MIDI melody is required; ambiguous, overlapping, bent or incomplete references and MP3/WAV audio without a melody reference are not scored. Complete target notes remain in coverage even when input is missing. Unreliable or insufficient input produces no reportable score. A natural song ending can show one estimate for that reservation and singer; manually stopping or replacing playback does not create a completion score. The last result is held in memory for this app session. No singing recording is stored or transmitted. The app cannot distinguish a singer from backing music playing the same melody. Input timing is estimated from dry frames and the audible playback clock; the adjustable timing offset needs physical calibration. Changing timing, key or pace clears accumulated observations. Scores are informal estimates, not an assessment of a person's ability.

CSV exports contain unencrypted song metadata and no audio. Private .kanta-book exports contain encrypted metadata and require their separate access code. Anyone with both file and code can read the titles; CSV/print copies are not encrypted. Sharing is an explicit action through the system picker. The website reader decrypts in the browser without uploading your file, code or searches.
