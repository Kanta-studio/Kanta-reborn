FEATURE GUIDE · OCTOBER 2026

More songs. More ways to sing.

These source features have automated test and simulator coverage, including the original starter-song integration. They still require acceptance testing on actual iPhones/iPads before release.

New: Illustrated iPhone & iPad setup guide

Follow our visual step-by-step KANTA guide to connect a microphone, compatible USB interface, wired headphones, mixer and amplifier, then configure vocal effects and experimental MIT-licensed Soundpipe PitchCorrect. Other chapters cover import, Songbook, Remote, scoring and party features. The new DSP path has not yet completed physical-device audio testing.

Three KANTA-original offline starter songs

Morning Light, Sing Together and Starlight Echo are short original KAR demos with synchronized lyrics and a small KANTA-generated instrument tone. Find them in My Set on first install, then Play, Queue, Remove or Restore offline. Other MIDI/KAR files continue to use imported SoundFonts; no licensed third-party song catalog is bundled.

Actual three-song sampler playback, timed lyrics, queue, seek, removal and restore across a process restart passed Swift Core CI #242. Signed TestFlight and physical-device audio have not been validated.

Song Library & searchable Songbook

Bring your own MIDI/KAR (with an SF2 SoundFont), MP3, or WAV files from Files or iCloud Drive. A user-selected, structurally recognized title index can be searchable without a playable media container. Unsupported entries show Catalog only; Reserve remains disabled.

  • Private on-device SQLite/FTS5 metadata catalog with bounded paging for large libraries.
  • Search song names, available artists and codes in Host CONTROL or an authenticated LAN Remote.
  • The optional KANTA Songbook shows title, local reference code, known artist and availability; it can export a complete metadata-only CSV using the iOS Share sheet.
  • For indexed titles, five-digit KANTA reference codes are based on one-based record position (e.g. 00001). These are not verified original song-provider codes; codes can change if the index is reordered, and may repeat across different libraries.
  • When the source does not supply an artist, KANTA shows “Artist not provided.” You may enter an artist yourself, which is stored locally and included in future search/export results. No artist is automatically guessed.

Private website Songbook — phone access

Inside the KANTA iOS app, open Songbook → Create private website copy and share an encrypted .kanta-book file. Enter the unique code shown on that phone at the private Songbook page. The reader flips pages, supports title/artist/code search, and can download CSV or print/save PDF. The website is an empty shell: no catalog, encrypted file, code or search terms are uploaded. The long code is not the six-digit LAN Remote PIN and does not expire or remotely revoke old exports. Downloaded CSV/PDF is plaintext, so protect copies.

Favorites & session replay

While singing, KANTA records the songs actually started—not merely songs in your queue. When you choose End Session, KANTA offers to save the performed set as a named Favorites collection. Saved collections can be renamed, deleted and loaded back into Up Next from Home or Host.

Favorites are device-local metadata, not audio copies. At reload time, KANTA checks the current authorized library and skips missing or catalog-only songs. An existing queue is preserved rather than erased.

Lyrics, visuals & playback

  • Fullscreen portrait and landscape stage with large centered, bold two/three-line synced lyrics where source timing exists; previous/current/next syllable or line highlighting depends on the file.
  • Subtle KANTA branding at the lower right of the singing stage and optional built-in background stills, procedural motion, or user-selected background assets.
  • FIFO Reserve/Up Next queue, transport controls, volume, pitch and tempo.
  • Opt-in on-device microphone-based pitch scoring where compatible song melody targets exist; it does not record or upload singing audio.
  • Stage/media rendering is designed to adapt across iPhone/iPad, including iOS/iPadOS 17+ supported hardware.

Host CONTROL & paired Remote

The main device has the complete Host CONTROL panel. A second iPhone/iPad may join over the same trusted local Wi-Fi/LAN, discover the Host, enter its six-digit PIN, search the Host's local song catalog, reserve songs and control playback. No separate copy of the Songbook is needed on the Remote.

Security note: the current paired LAN stream is PIN-authorized but not encrypted. Do not treat it as safe for public or untrusted Wi-Fi.

Vocal Soundcard (live microphone FX)

Host → Soundcard · Vocal FX provides Dry/Clean, Small Room, Studio Vocal, Concert Hall, Cathedral, Plate Reverb and Echo/Delay presets, with adjustable Reverb, Echo, Bass, Treble and Mic Level.

These effects apply only to microphone audio that actually enters the iOS device. Vocal monitoring is off by default and gated to a supported wired-headphone, USB-audio or line-out route. Built-in-speaker, Bluetooth and AirPlay live monitoring is muted for feedback/latency safety. A microphone plugged directly into an external amplifier bypasses KANTA's FX. Scoring reads the dry input; backing tracks do not pass through this vocal chain.

Actual mic-to-amplifier latency, sound quality, routing interruptions and feedback protection require real-hardware acceptance; the simulator build is not proof of usable low-latency monitoring.

Vocal polish · Phase 1

Alongside the existing Room, Studio, Hall, Cathedral, Plate and Echo environments, KANTA now includes native compression, downward expander/noise gate, adaptive high-frequency de-essing, a short-delay vocal doubler, adjustable echo time and an output peak limiter. Ready-made OPM Ballad, Pop Star, Rock Concert, Duet, Studio Clean and Party Karaoke presets provide quick starting points. These processors act only on microphone input entering KANTA; the scoring tap remains dry.

The Soundcard is not an external hardware DSP. Live mic monitor remains opt-in, muted on unsafe routes, and needs wired/USB hardware latency and feedback testing before device release.

Party SFX Pad · Phase 2

Eight KANTA-original, procedurally generated one-shot sounds: Applause, Cheering, Drum Roll, Air Horn, Laughter, Ta-Da, Ding and Sad Trombone. Host can trigger, stop and adjust SFX volume without changing the song queue; up to four one-shots can overlap. A long-press on a pad opens an import action for a short audio clip from Files, which is stored only in the app's private storage. Restore original returns to the KANTA-authored sound.

A PIN-paired Remote can trigger and stop these same Host effects on trusted LAN. Custom samples never transfer to the Remote. Soundboard audio is separate from vocal monitoring and dry mic scoring.

Phase 3 · Coming later

An experimental, default-off monophonic pitch-correction control is implemented as an experimental default-off control in the iOS source for key/scale note snapping, with wired/USB-only monitor safety and unchanged dry scoring. It has not passed device or latency testing and is not yet a released app capability. Vocal harmonizer and tempo-aware harmonies remain disabled future placeholders.

Offline use, Files & iCloud Save

Routine karaoke does not require a developer-operated account or streaming catalog. Files/iCloud Drive folders are user-selected; original files are kept under their provider's control. Optional iCloud Save prepares selected app settings/media categories for Apple's iCloud service; local preparation does not guarantee cloud upload completion. Only the three short first-party original KAR demos and generated instrument tone are bundled; no imported user content or third-party song catalog is supplied.

Availability: KANTA is preparing internal testing; public App Store distribution is not available yet. Source-level implementation and simulator CI success are distinct from signed TestFlight and real-device acceptance.

Stage Display · dedicated TV/projector output

On compatible iPhones and iPads, KANTA can place a separate synchronized karaoke stage (lyrics, backgrounds and subtle branding) on an HDMI/USB-C connected TV or on an AirPlay destination for which iOS provides an independent external-display scene. The main device keeps queue search, singer controls and Vocal FX. The connected display never owns a second playback queue.

Not every AirPlay device supports extended displays: some routes mirror the phone instead, including its controls. Real display detection, lyrics timing and hardware routing still require acceptance testing.

Microphone & audio input manager

Inspect the active input and output devices, available microphones, sample rate, estimated I/O buffer duration and live dry input level. Choose a preferred available microphone or return to system default and adjust scoring latency. Diagnostics share KANTA's single on-device capture engine; they do not record or upload audio. Hardware buffer duration is not a measured microphone-to-speaker round trip.

Singer queue and editable playlists

Assign optional singer names to Host or paired-Remote song reservations; move each queued song up or down without losing its singer label. Create editable named playlists under Saved Favorites, add songs from paged local search, remove/reorder them and load them into the same Up Next queue. KANTA still checks that source media exists and is authorized before playback; catalog-only records cannot play.

Automatic between-song music

Choose your own supported audio file to play as looping intermission music while Host playback is stopped. KANTA stores the selected track locally without transcoding, provides independent volume, and fades it out automatically when karaoke begins. Intermission audio never enters your song queue or uploads to the developer.

Coming later · Vocal Fit and advanced singing tools

Optional voice-range checks, melody-supported song recommendations, transposition suggestions, quiz modes and real-time harmonies are future concepts, not present release features. Experimental monophonic pitch correction is present in the app source but default-off and not a publicly shipped or device-validated capability. Song-title-only indexes cannot provide verified vocal ranges without melody evidence.

🎤 Filipino + English Voice Search — upcoming app update

KANTA's in-development iOS update adds optional on-device Filipino/Taglish recognition using a multilingual Whisper Base language pack. Download once (approximately 147 MB plus tokenizer/cache) from Settings or Songbook; a microphone permission is needed only when you tap Voice Search. Speak actual song titles, artists or numeric codes and review the recognized search before pressing Reserve. English (PH) remains available through Apple's Speech recognition, which may process voice online. User media and speech recordings are not sent to a KANTA server. Support, reliability, and 90% accuracy still require real-device validation before release.
