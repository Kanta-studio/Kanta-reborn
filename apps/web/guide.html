GETTING STARTED

From your library
to your first chorus.

Start with the version for your device. Android is a debug testing APK; iPhone and iPad require personal signing of the unsigned IPA.

Android downloadiPhone / iPad download

Your first song

  1. Install the appropriate testing build and open KANTA.
  2. Choose an original starter song: Morning Light, Sing Together or Starlight Echo.
  3. Reserve or play it, follow the lyrics and try the volume, tempo and pitch controls.
  4. Add a singer name and reserve another song to try Up Next.

Add your own music

Use the app's file picker for supported MIDI/KAR, MP3/WAV and an SF2 SoundFont. Android also supports folder import and cancellation. Selected originals remain unchanged; the app uses its own private copies. MIDI/KAR needs a compatible SoundFont to produce sound.

Open your private Songbook

  1. Export an encrypted Songbook from KANTA.
  2. Open My Songbook and choose that file.
  3. Enter its separate access code locally in your browser.
  4. Search your titles, then export CSV or print if wanted.

Keep the code separate from the file. Anyone with both can read the titles. No song audio is included.

Microphone and soundcard

The recovered iOS app has optional live microphone and vocal tools, but physical audio acceptance is pending. Use the app's opt-in controls and avoid speaker feedback. The current Android download has no microphone monitoring, effects or scoring. A microphone is not needed to play the original starters.

Your Android stage

In Scenic, choose a photo or muted background video through Android Files. Originals remain unchanged; the private selection is retained for reuse. You can also turn on front-camera preview after granting camera permission. Nothing is recorded, and leaving KANTA turns the camera off. Camera preview moves between the main and fullscreen stage; TV uses a still background.

Remote control

Android companion networking is not in the current download. The recovered iOS remote is a legacy PIN-authorized, unencrypted local-network feature; use only on a trusted network. Encrypted cross-platform pairing is not available.

If a song will not play

Confirm the media is actually available, the format is supported, and a compatible SF2 is selected for MIDI/KAR. Check device volume and output routing. Catalog-only rows cannot be queued. Keep a separate copy of valuable originals before testing.

Testing preview

These are recovered development builds, not store releases. Android emulator checks and iPhone/iPad simulator walkthroughs passed. Physical-device audio quality, microphone latency, camera, HDMI and AirPlay acceptance remain open. YouTube streaming, ad filtering, Vocal Fit and harmonies are not included.
