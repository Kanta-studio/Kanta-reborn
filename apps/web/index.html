How to use KANTA

Same flow on every device — import, reserve, perform.

1

Import your songs

Open the Library, choose a folder or pick MIDI/KAR/MP3/WAV files. KANTA parses the lyrics and builds your set list.

Both
2

Reserve turns

Add singers to Up Next, reorder the queue, and save sets so the next night is ready to go.

Both
3

Take the stage

Launch full-screen mode with timed lyrics, stage controls and optional vocal tools. Sing.

iOS • iPadOS

iPhone & iPad

iOS 17 or later. Native SwiftUI stage, full-screen karaoke player, external-display TV stage, encrypted companion remote, and optional vocal tools. Signed with personal signing — install directly on your device.

✔ Library import & saved sets
✔ Timed, highlighted lyrics
✔ Full-screen & TV stage

Android

Android

Android 8 or later. Native Java/Kotlin player with TinySoundFont synthesis, timed SMF lyrics, personal media folders, opt-in camera preview, party pads and encrypted companion control.

✔ MIDI/KAR & MP3/WAV playback
✔ SAF folder & SF2 import
✔ Stage, party & intermission
✔ Opt-in mic monitoring & FX
