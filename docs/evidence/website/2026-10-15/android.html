Built for your karaoke night.

♪

Use your own songs

Import supported MIDI/KAR or MP3/WAV from Android Files. Local copies and song metadata are kept in app-private storage; your original source files are left alone.

▣

Lyrics and stage

Stage-first song playback, synced KAR lyrics, Scenic user photos/videos and optional live front-camera preview. An extended-display stage can follow selected images or muted user video behind lyrics in source; HDMI hardware acceptance remains pending.

⌕

Remote-style controls

Search by code, title or artist, use the number pad and RES, or opt in to Android voice recognition. The new Android test version adds private Wi-Fi companion control between Android phones. Share the Host's pairing QR code or invitation privately. Two-phone real-world testing is still in progress; iPhone and Android cannot pair with each other yet.

♡

Queue and saved sets

Singer-labeled FIFO reservations, Favorites and reusable saved sessions. Export a simple song list, or save a private, access-code-protected Songbook file. Keep the code separate from the file; sharing both allows someone else to open the list.

♫

Live mic and SoundFont

Original SoundFont or user-selected SF2, optional local microphone monitoring, experimental pitch correction, dry pitch scoring and party effects. Audio quality and latency still need real-device acceptance.

⚖

Legal and privacy in-app

Bring your own licensed files, understand public performance rules and optional sensor permissions. Android app disclosures are readable offline from CONTROL; full KANTA policies are linked below.

Your library stays your responsibility.

KANTA does not bundle commercial music, record live camera video or upload microphone recordings to the KANTA website. Android voice recognition uses your chosen device speech provider, which may send voice to its own services. The private Songbook feature is included in this new testing version, but has not yet been checked between real iOS and Android devices. The Android companion remote has passed its first encrypted-pairing test on an emulator; two real phones and iPhone/Android pairing still need testing. Offline Filipino/Taglish voice search is not ready.
