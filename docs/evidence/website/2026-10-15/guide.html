01 / PREPARE YOUR SETUP

What you'll need

Choose one of the two configurations below. Soundcard effects only process audio that actually enters KANTA through the iPhone or iPad microphone input.

📱

iPhone with Lightning

For Lightning iPhones such as iPhone XR, use a compatible Lightning USB camera/host adapter, preferably powered, when connecting a class-compliant USB audio interface.

Do not assume an ordinary charging cable can connect a USB microphone/interface.
▯

USB-C iPhone or iPad

Use a compatible USB-C audio interface, or a powered USB-C hub with USB audio support. Older Lightning iPads require an appropriate host adapter instead.

USB-C support does not guarantee every interface, hub or microphone works.
🎚

Microphone + interface

Recommended: a class-compliant USB audio interface with a microphone input, gain knob, and stereo/headphone/line output. Connect a dynamic mic or a supported condenser mic.

Condenser microphones may require 48 V phantom power from the interface—not the phone.
🔌

Output + monitoring

Use wired headphones for setup, or connect the interface line output to a mixer, powered speakers or amplifier line input. Start all volumes low.

Never connect an amplifier speaker output to a phone or audio-interface input.

Important: KANTA accepts live monitored output only on compatible wired headphones, USB audio or line-out routes. Its built-in speaker, Bluetooth and AirPlay outputs are muted for live microphone monitoring. A mic plugged directly into a mixer bypasses KANTA's processing.

1

Connect and authorize your mic

Open the Host and find the Soundcard/Live Vocal FX controls. Allow microphone permission when asked. Verify the input and output in the Audio Input Manager.

2

Enable live microphone output

Turn on Live microphone to sound output. Only proceed when Monitor reports an allowed wired/USB/line-out route. If muted, resolve the route first.

3

Choose a sound

Start with Dry / Clean or Studio Vocal. Adjust Compression, Noise Gate, De-Esser, Doubler, Reverb, Echo, Bass, Treble and Mic Level gradually.

4

Enable MIT pitch correction

Turn on Pitch Correction (experimental). Choose Soundpipe · PitchCorrect for the MIT-licensed correction effect, or Apple · Native for the fallback. Match your song's key and scale.

5

Tune gently, then sing

Try modest Correction Amount and Response Speed first, listening through wired headphones at low volume. Increase for a more synthetic tuning effect. Lower or disable it if you hear artifacts or delay.

Key & scale

A matching key helps the tuner choose musically correct notes. Wrong scales can make a good vocal sound wrong.

Dry scoring

Scoring observes the unprocessed microphone signal, not the Soundcard's corrected output. Scoring needs compatible melody targets.

Not zero latency

DSP, USB interfaces and adapters add delay. Hardware quality and stability are still under evaluation.

04 / DO A SAFE SOUND CHECK

Five-minute first-run checklist

  1. Power down the loud output. Connect all cables first; set the interface, KANTA Mic Level and amplifier/mixer faders low.
  2. Confirm device routes. In KANTA's Audio Input Manager, verify that the microphone is actually visible and the current output is wired headphones, USB audio or line-out.
  3. Check raw microphone activity. Speak/sing and look for input-level feedback. If it stays flat, check interface gain, permission and the selected mic.
  4. Turn on monitoring carefully. Enable live output, then raise the volume slowly while listening for echo, distortion, feedback or delay.
  5. Try a single effect. Start Dry; add a little Room reverb, then optional Soundpipe tuning. When finished, turn monitoring off and verify output mutes.

Volume safety: Never point a live microphone at a nearby loudspeaker. Keep feedback protection, gain staging and speaker placement in mind. If a route changes or feedback begins, mute the mixer and disable monitoring immediately.

05 / MAKE THE MOST OF KANTA

More feature walkthroughs

📁

Import songs & SoundFonts

  1. Open My Set / Library and choose an import or folder option.
  2. Select your own MIDI/KAR, MP3 or WAV files; add a supported .sf2 for MIDI/KAR playback.
  3. Wait for indexing and search by title, artist or KANTA code.
Unsupported catalog-only items cannot be queued. Original files remain yours.
📖

Private Songbook

  1. Open Songbook in KANTA and choose Create private website copy.
  2. Save/share its encrypted .kanta-book file.
  3. On the website, choose the file and enter the long code from the phone.
CSV and printed PDF copies are unencrypted; keep them private.
📺

Fullscreen karaoke lyrics

  1. Reserve a playable song to Up Next.
  2. Start playback and open fullscreen for large synchronized lyrics.
  3. Choose supported Scenic/Realm visuals and an external Stage Display when available.
Some files have no timing or guide-melody data.
📲

Companion Remote

  1. Keep Host and Remote devices on the same trusted local Wi-Fi.
  2. On Host, display the pairing PIN.
  3. On the second device, discover Host and enter that PIN to control search/queue.
PIN pairing is not encryption. Only use trusted LANs.
⭐

Score & save Favorites

  1. Enable optional scoring and allow mic permission.
  2. Choose a song with a valid, unambiguous melody target.
  3. When finished, save played songs as a named Favorites set to replay later.
Scoring uses dry mic audio; unsupported tracks remain playable but unscored.
🥁

Party SFX and stage tools

  1. Open the SFX pad in Host or paired Remote.
  2. Trigger one of eight original generated effects or choose your own short clip.
  3. Use Stop All; adjust dedicated SFX output independently.
Harmonizer and automatic harmony effects are planned, not active.

06 / TROUBLESHOOTING

What to check when sound isn't right

Expand the symptom you are experiencing.

Monitor says muted / no vocal output

Confirm microphone permission, live monitor toggle, and an allowed wired headphones / USB audio / line-out output. The device speaker, Bluetooth and AirPlay are intentionally blocked for live mic monitoring. Unplug/reconnect safely and check the Audio Input Manager; restart monitoring only after the route is stable.

The microphone works on the mixer, but KANTA effects do nothing

The mic must feed an audio input recognized by iPhone/iPad. An analog mixer-to-speaker path does not pass through KANTA. Use a supported USB audio interface to send the microphone to the device and return processed output to a safe mixer line input. Avoid routing the returned signal back into the mic input.

Delay, doubled voice or echo

First turn Echo and Vocal Doubler down, then disable pitch correction. Avoid Bluetooth and AirPlay for live monitoring. Test wired headphones at low level, reduce interface buffers where supported, and compare Soundpipe with Apple Native. Real-device round-trip latency is not yet certified.

Feedback, screeching or distorted sound

Immediately lower the amplifier/interface gains and disable live monitoring. Move speakers away from microphones. Lower Mic Level, reverb, and input preamp gain. Never send a speaker-level output into a mic/line input.

Voice sounds off-key or overly robotic

Check Key and Scale, then reduce Correction Amount and Response Speed. Not every song's key can be inferred from metadata. Test with one singer at a time; monophonic correction is not a duet harmonizer.

Audio stops when I connect a screen or another accessory

HDMI, USB adapters and hubs can change iOS audio routes. Recheck the input/output. Stage Display connectivity does not guarantee that live mic monitoring can run through HDMI; unsupported routes remain muted for safety.

Imported songs have no score or synchronized lyrics

Playback may be supported even when lyric timing or a single trustworthy melody guide is missing. Import compatible KAR/MIDI with timing and a clear melody target, or enjoy the song without score. Do not assume KANTA can infer unavailable lyrics or copyrighted song data.

07 / HELP US FIX BUGS

Record a private technical report

No microphone recording, song titles or PINs are needed to identify a safe-route failure. Enable diagnostics only if you want a short-lived local event history.

1

Opt in

Open KANTA Settings → Support & Diagnostic Reports and turn on Keep local diagnostic events.

2

Reproduce the error

Connect your interface and follow the exact action that failed. KANTA stores a maximum of 200 redacted event codes over seven days, only on your device.

3

Review and choose where to send it

Tap Prepare support report, then Review / Share report. Sharing is a separate choice; KANTA never sends reports automatically. Turn off diagnostics or tap Erase afterward.

4

Separate crash reports

If KANTA closes unexpectedly, Apple system crash data may be useful. In iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Analytics Data, select the relevant report yourself. Review its contents before sharing.

For private test installs: BetaDrop hosts an already Apple-signed IPA. It does not sign apps or bypass device registration. A short-lived password-protected link is safer than a widely shared beta link. Read the support reporting guide.
