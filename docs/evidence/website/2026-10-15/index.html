BRING YOUR OWN LIBRARY

Music stays under your control.

Choose your songs in Apple's Files app. KANTA includes three short original KAR samples with a generated instrument tone. For your own library, KANTA supports compatible MIDI/KAR with your own SF2 SoundFont and MP3/WAV audio. Supported title indexes can be searchable even when matching media is unavailable; those rows stay clearly marked Catalog only and cannot be queued for playback.
