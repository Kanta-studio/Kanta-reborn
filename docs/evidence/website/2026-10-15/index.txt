HOST AND COMPANION REMOTE
Party SFX Pad
Eight original KANTA sound buttons, one-shot Stop All, separate effects volume, and optional owner-provided short clip imports. A PIN-paired Remote can trigger sounds on the Host. The source includes optional experimental monophonic pitch correction (off by default); harmonizers remain future placeholders. Real-device quality and latency are not validated.

BRING YOUR OWN LIBRARY
Music stays under your control.
Choose your songs in Apple's Files app. KANTA includes three short original KAR samples with a generated instrument tone. For your own library, KANTA supports compatible MIDI/KAR with your own SF2 SoundFont and MP3/WAV audio.
